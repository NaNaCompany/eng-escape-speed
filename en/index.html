FORMULA

v_esc = √(2GM/r)

Assumptions

Required: M,r>0 and an exterior spherical Newtonian gravity field. Use center distance, not altitude. Excluded: atmosphere, rotation, other bodies and continued thrust. Results at or above light speed are rejected.

Displayed values are floating-point approximations. Design safety factors and unprovided error contributions are not calculated.
